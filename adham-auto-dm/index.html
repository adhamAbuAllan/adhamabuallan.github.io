Privacy Policy – Adham Auto DM

Last updated: 30 September 2026 · Operated by Adham Abu Allan · adhamallan4@gmail.com

Adham Auto DM lets Instagram professional accounts ("businesses") answer comments automatically. When someone comments a keyword on the business's post, the business replies and sends that person a direct message with the link they asked for. It may first ask them to follow the business, and the business may ask for an email address before sending the link. It is built only on Meta's official Instagram API.

1. People who comment on or message a business that uses Adham Auto DM

We collect:

  • Your Instagram-scoped user ID, username and display name
  • The text and ID of the comment you left
  • Your taps on the buttons in our messages
  • Whether you follow the business, checked through Instagram's API only after you message the business or tap a button
  • Your email address, only if you choose to type it (you can skip when the business allows it)
  • Whether you opened the link we sent (a short redirect counts the click)

We use this only to reply to your comment, send you the link you asked for, send at most one reminder within 24 hours if you were asked to follow, avoid duplicates, and show the business totals and the emails people chose to share with it.

2. Businesses that connect their Instagram account

We collect the account ID, username and name, the access token Instagram issues when you connect, and the campaigns you set up (keywords, links, messages). The token is used only to send the replies and messages your campaigns describe, check follow status, and list your posts in your dashboard.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

In a private database hosted by Supabase (EU region), accessible only to the operator.

How long we keep it

No longer than 12 months; older records are deleted automatically.

Delete your data

  • Send the word DELETE in a direct message to the business account: everything about you is erased immediately.
  • Businesses: remove "Adham Auto DM" in Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites. Your account, token and campaigns are deleted automatically.
  • Or email adhamallan4@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your Instagram username (erased within 30 days).

See the data deletion instructions.
