1. The service

Adham Auto DM lets owners of Instagram professional accounts ("you") set up keyword campaigns: when someone comments a keyword on your post, the service replies and sends that person a direct message with a link you chose, optionally after they follow you or share an email address. It works only through Meta's official Instagram API and only on accounts you connect yourself with Instagram login.

  • Your links and messages must be lawful and must not be misleading, harmful or spam.
  • If you collect email addresses, you are responsible for using them lawfully and only for the purpose you told people about.

3. What the service does not do

It never posts content on your behalf other than replies to comments on your own posts, never messages people who did not comment or write to you first, and never sells or shares data. See the Privacy Policy.
