Data Deletion Instructions – Adham Auto DM

If you commented on or messaged a business that uses Adham Auto DM

  1. Send the word DELETE in a direct message to that business on Instagram. Everything linked to your account (user ID, username, name, comments, button taps, follow-check results, email, link clicks) is erased immediately and you get a confirmation reply.

If you connected your business account

  1. In Instagram go to Settings → Apps and websites and remove Adham Auto DM. Your account, access token, campaigns and all their data are deleted automatically, and you receive a confirmation code.

Or by email

Email adhamallan4@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your Instagram username. We erase all records within 30 days and confirm by reply.
